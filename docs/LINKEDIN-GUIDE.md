# LinkedIn Optimization Playbook

Use `/opus linkedin` to get Claude to review and rewrite your LinkedIn profile. This guide explains the strategy behind the recommendations.

---

## The 4 High-Impact Areas

### 1. Headline (120 characters)

Your headline is the highest-weight text for LinkedIn's search algorithm AND the first thing recruiters read.

**Formula:** `[What You Do] | [Industry/Domain] | [Specific Value or Tool]`

**Bad:** "Experienced Professional Seeking New Opportunities"
**Good:** "Operations Manager | Supply Chain & Logistics | Lean Six Sigma Black Belt"
**Great:** "Senior FP&A Manager | SaaS & Enterprise Finance | Built $0→$500M Forecasting Models"

Rules:
- Include 2–3 keywords recruiters search for
- Lead with your current/target title
- No buzzwords ("passionate", "results-driven", "thought leader")
- No "Seeking new opportunities" — it signals desperation and wastes keyword space

### 2. About Section (2,600 chars max)

LinkedIn shows only the first ~300 characters before "See more" — make them count.

**Structure:**
1. **Hook** (1 sentence): Start with a strong statement about your impact or specialty
2. **What you do** (2–3 sentences): Your core expertise and the problems you solve
3. **How you do it** (1–2 sentences): Your approach, methodology, or philosophy
4. **Proof** (2–3 bullet points): Specific achievements with numbers
5. **What's next** (1 sentence): What you're looking for
6. **CTA** (1 sentence): "Open to [role type] opportunities — connect or message me"

Rules:
- Write in first person ("I built...", not "John is a...")
- Include your top 5–8 target keywords naturally
- Use line breaks and spacing — walls of text get skipped
- Include at least 2 quantified achievements

### 3. Experience Bullets

Each role needs 3–5 bullets. Apply the same ATS keyword logic as your resume.

**Formula:** `[Action verb] + [what you did] + [result with metric]`

**Bad:** "Responsible for managing projects"
**Good:** "Led cross-functional teams of 12 to deliver $3.2M ERP migration 6 weeks ahead of schedule"

Rules:
- Start every bullet with a strong action verb (Led, Built, Reduced, Launched, Managed, Negotiated)
- Include at least one metric per bullet (%, $, # of people, time saved, revenue generated)
- Mirror keywords from target job descriptions

### 4. Skills Section (top 50)

LinkedIn lets you list up to 50 skills. Recruiter searches weight endorsed skills heavily.

**Strategy:**
1. List skills that appear most frequently in your target JDs first
2. Ask colleagues and managers to endorse your top 10
3. Include both formal titles ("Project Management") and tools ("Tableau", "Salesforce")
4. Include certifications as skills ("PMP", "Six Sigma", "CPA")

## Open to Work Settings

Set these strategically:
- **Job titles**: List 3–5 exact titles (use the same wording recruiters search)
- **Locations**: Be specific — "New York City Metropolitan Area" not just "New York"
- **Start date**: "Immediately" signals urgency; "Flexible" is safer if currently employed
- **Visibility**: "All LinkedIn members" → seen by recruiters AND your current employer
  "Recruiters only" → LinkedIn adds a green badge visible only to premium recruiters

---

## Activity & Engagement (Bonus Impact)

- Post once a week about your industry, a project, or a professional insight
- Comment thoughtfully on posts from people in your target companies
- Connect with recruiters at target companies before you apply — it significantly improves InMail response rates
- Follow target companies to see their posts and demonstrate engagement

- [ ] Complete About section (500+ words)
- [ ] All experience entries have bullets
- [ ] Education section complete
- [ ] 10+ skills listed with endorsements
- [ ] At least 3 recommendations
