# Mode: Outreach

Triggered by `/opus outreach` or "write a LinkedIn message" / "draft outreach to recruiter" / "follow-up message".

## Types

### Cold Outreach (recruiter or hiring manager)
**Goal:** Get noticed before or alongside an application.

Template:
```
Hi [Name],

I came across [Company]'s [Role] opening and was immediately drawn to [specific detail about the company or team].

I'm a [title] with [X years] in [domain], and I've [one-line achievement most relevant to this role]. I'd love to connect and learn more about the team.

[Your name]
```
Rules:
- Max 3 sentences of substance
- Always reference something specific (not "I'm interested in your company")
- Never ask "Do you have 30 minutes?" — just ask to connect
- Include your LinkedIn profile URL only if relevant

### Application Follow-Up (after applying)
**Goal:** Signal genuine interest and stand out from mass applicants.

Template (send 5–7 business days after applying):
```
Hi [Name],

I submitted my application for [Role] last week and wanted to reiterate my interest. 

[One sentence: specific thing that excites you about the role/company + one relevant credential]

Happy to share more context on my background if useful.

[Your name]
```

### Post-Interview Thank You
Send within 24 hours of each interview.
- Reference a specific moment from the conversation
- Reinforce one key point from your strongest answer
- Express genuine enthusiasm (not generic)

## Steps

1. **Identify contact** — ask for name, role, company, platform (LinkedIn/email)
2. **Load context** — use the evaluation report for that company (if it exists)
3. **Draft message** — using the appropriate template + personalization
4. **Present draft** — show the full message and ask for approval
5. **User approves** → copy to clipboard or display for manual sending
6. **NEVER send messages directly** — always present for user review first
